Root Cause Analysis

Why Existing Platforms Fail
the Niche MSME Market

1

Marketplaces Penalize Identity, Reward Scale

Large marketplaces (Shopee, Tokopedia) have over 15M sellers, with discovery algorithms favoring high-budget advertisers. Small heritage stores like Toko Sakinah become invisible. Commission rates of 5–12% per transaction further squeeze already thin margins.

Shopee fee: up to 12% per category (Source: Shopee Seller Center, 2024)
2

Online Apparel Shopping Is a Sizing Gamble

Without physical try-on, consumers default to "best guess" sizing, driving the 20–40% return rate. Premium brands deploy AR/VR fitting (Zara, ASOS) requiring $10M+ investments. This technology gap leaves MSMEs unable to compete on shopper confidence.

ASOS reportedly invested £100M+ in virtual fitting (Source: Retail Week, 2023)
3

B2B Custom Orders Drown in WhatsApp Chaos

Schools and institutions order uniforms in bulk via fragmented WhatsApp threads. There's no structured platform for spec negotiation, formal quotations, deposit tracking, or production timelines — leading to miscommunication, disputes, and lost deals.

WhatsApp Business: 200M+ users in Indonesia (Meta, 2023)

THE GAP: No platform combines niche-focused branding for MSMEs, affordable AI sizing technology, and structured B2B custom-order management — until now.

STEM / Technical Application

Engineering the Affordable AI:
Math, Computer Vision, and Secure Architecture

Applied Mathematics

  • Anthropometric ratio: chest = shoulder × 2.4 (validated against industry sizing tables)
  • Camera calibration: face width = 14.5 cm reference for pixel-to-cm conversion
  • Moving-average smoothing across 30 frames to stabilize measurements

Computer Vision Science

  • MediaPipe Pose: 33-landmark real-time body detection (WebAssembly)
  • Visibility filtering: only landmarks >0.6 confidence trigger measurement
  • Trade-off: client-side ML = privacy preserved, no GPU server cost

Systems Engineering

  • 9-table relational schema (3NF normalized for transactional integrity)
  • SHA-512 HMAC signature verification on payment webhooks
  • .htaccess hardening + MIME validation on every file upload

Social Impact · Scalability · Leadership

Beyond Revenue:
Building an Inclusive Digital Economy

Social Impact

  • Each onboarded MSME adds 1–3 local jobs (admin, packer, courier liaison)
  • ~42 million still-offline MSMEs (KemenkopUKM 2023) become addressable
  • Owners aged 50+ adopt digital tools through guided onboarding
  • Estimated IDR 100K–200K savings per child/year avoiding mis-sized uniforms
  • Pilgrimage market: 1.7M+ travelers/year access verified halal product catalogs

Scalability

  • Vertical: uniforms → Muslim fashion → halal cosmetics → packaged foods
  • Geographic: Indonesia (Y1) → Malaysia, Brunei (Y2) — shared school uniform culture
  • Tech: AR overlay (Y2), voice ordering in Bahasa (Y2), ML stock prediction (Y3)
  • Partnership: integration with 130K+ Indonesian cooperatives (Kop. Indonesia)
  • Architecture supports multi-tenant rollout — engineered for Day-1 horizontal scaling

What I Gained

  • Customer empathy first: spent days understanding owner workflows before writing code
  • Engineering pragmatism: chose client-side AI over costly server ML — a financial-engineering decision, not just technical
  • Production discipline: learned to debug live (auth_check upload error, CSS specificity bug) under user-visible conditions
  • Strategic narrowing: resisted scope creep — niche > vertical breadth
  • Solo-founder accountability: every layer (UX, backend, payments, deploy) was mine to own end-to-end

The most important lesson wasn't technical. It was learning that shipping imperfect but real > demoing flawless but fictional — and that empathy for the user is the cheapest, highest-leverage engineering tool we have.
